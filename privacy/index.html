Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Mobile.Deals ("we", "us") collects when you use mobile.deals and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (for example the Bill Audit, deal alerts, business quote, trade-in offers, contest entry, job application, pledge or contact form) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and your answers. Forms are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to your request, send what you asked for, run contests and improve our service.

Information collected automatically

Our pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Your browser stores a few preferences on your device (theme, saved deals and cookie notice choice) using local storage; this data never leaves your device.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent message where required.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click play. Links to deals take you to third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the site (such as form processing), when required by law, or with your permission (for example to obtain trade-in or business quotes you requested).

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them, then delete them. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. California residents may request that we not sell or share personal information; we do not sell it. To make a request, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

Mobile.Deals is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
