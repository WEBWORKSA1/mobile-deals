FAQ

How is the Deal Radar calculated?

Every 6 hours we scan the titles of all live deals and count mentions of current phone models. A higher count means more retailers and carriers are discounting that model right now.

Should I buy last year's flagship?

Often yes. Previous flagships tend to drop sharply in price after a new launch while still getting years of software updates.
