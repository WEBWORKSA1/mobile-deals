How the audit works

  1. Your bill. We compare your total monthly cost with every plan in our Plan Finder that meets your data, network and hotspot needs.
  2. Your savings. We calculate the gap per month and per year using published multi-line pricing where available.
  3. Your report. If you ask for it, we email the best options and the steps to switch without losing your number.

FAQ

Do I need to upload my bill?

No. Your total monthly amount and number of lines are enough for an accurate estimate.

What if my bill includes phone payments?

Tell us in step 1. The report separates device payments from service so you can see the real service savings and when switching makes sense.

Is my information shared?

We use it only to send your report. We never sell personal information. See our Privacy Policy.
