Frequently asked questions

How does Mobile.Deals find deals?

An automated job scans public deal communities and publishers in the US, Canada, the UK, Europe, Australia and India every 6 hours, keeps only mobile-related offers and links you to the original source.

Is the Bill Audit really free?

Yes. You see your savings estimate instantly without giving any details. If you want the full personalised report, you share an email address and we send it. No spam, and we never sell your data.

How do you make money?

Through advertising, some affiliate links (we may earn a commission when you sign up through them, at no extra cost to you), sponsorships and reader support. Rankings in our tools are based on price and features. See our disclosures.

Which countries do you cover?

The live deals feed covers the US, Canada, the UK, Europe, Australia and India. The Plan Finder currently covers US plans, with more regions on the roadmap.
