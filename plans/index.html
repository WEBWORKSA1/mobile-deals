How we compare plans

Each plan lists the advertised monthly price per line, the network it runs on, the data allowance and the conditions attached (prepay terms, AutoPay or promotional periods). The 24-month total multiplies the monthly cost for your number of lines by 24 and uses published multi-line pricing where the carrier provides it. Cost per GB divides the monthly price by the data allowance, with unlimited plans treated as 50GB for comparison.

We verify prices against carrier websites and show the date of the last check. Some links are affiliate links; that never changes the order, which is determined only by your filters and sort choice. Read our disclosures.

FAQ

Why do some cheap plans require paying upfront?

Some prepaid carriers offer their lowest monthly rate only when you prepay several months or a full year. The effective monthly price is real, but you pay it in one go.

Do these prices include taxes?

Many prepaid plans include taxes and fees; postpaid plans usually add them on top. Check the carrier's broadband label for the exact amount.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Every carrier listed supports number porting. Follow our switching checklist.
