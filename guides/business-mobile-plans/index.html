Business Mobile Plans: How Small Teams Cut Phone Costs by 30%+

Pooled data, BYOD stipends and device management. A practical guide to choosing mobile service for a team of 5 to 500.

Updated November 19, 2026 · 6 min read · By the Mobile.Deals team

For a growing company, phones quietly become a large recurring cost. Many teams overpay because lines were added one at a time with no review. A structured approach usually finds savings of 20–40% without hurting coverage.

Three models for business mobile

  1. Company-paid lines: the business owns the account and devices. Maximum control, highest cost.
  2. BYOD with a stipend: employees use personal phones and receive a monthly allowance. Low admin, lower cost, less control.
  3. Hybrid: company lines for roles that need them (field staff, executives, on-call) and stipends for everyone else.

Where the savings come from

  • Right-sizing: most business lines use far less data than their plan allows. Audit usage and move light users down a tier.
  • Pooled or shared data: one bucket for the team instead of unlimited for everyone.
  • Removing zombie lines: lines for former employees or unused tablets are common.
  • Negotiated pricing: carriers offer business discounts at volume, especially at 10+ lines.
  • Device lifecycle: buying refurbished or keeping devices for three years cuts capital costs.

Security and management

A mobile device management (MDM) tool lets you enforce passcodes, separate work and personal data, and wipe company data from a lost phone. For BYOD, use work profiles so you never touch personal data.

A 30-day savings plan

  1. Export the last three bills and list every line, user and usage.
  2. Flag unused and low-usage lines.
  3. Decide the model (company, BYOD, hybrid) by role.
  4. Request quotes from at least three carriers or resellers.
  5. Migrate in waves, starting with low-risk roles.

FAQ

Is a business plan cheaper than consumer plans?

Not always per line, but business accounts add pooled data, account management and support, which matter at scale.

What stipend is typical for BYOD?

It varies by role and region. Base it on the actual cost of a suitable plan rather than a round number.

Are phone costs tax-deductible?

Business use is commonly deductible, but rules vary by country. Ask your accountant.
