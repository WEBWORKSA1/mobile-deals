The Best Time to Buy a Phone: A Month-by-Month Deal Calendar

Phone prices follow launch cycles and retail events. Time your purchase and you can save hundreds on the exact same phone.

Updated November 19, 2026 · 6 min read · By the Mobile.Deals team

Phone pricing is predictable. Manufacturers launch on a yearly rhythm, and retailers discount the outgoing model to clear stock. If you can wait a few weeks, you can often buy the same phone for much less.

The yearly rhythm

PeriodWhat usually happensStrategy
January–FebruarySamsung's Galaxy S launch window; previous S models discountedBuy last year's Galaxy S, or preorder bonuses on new
March–AprilMid-range launches (Galaxy A, Pixel a-series in some years)Good time for budget phones
JulyLarge online summer sales events; foldable launchesAccessories and previous-gen Android deals
AugustGoogle Pixel launch window; back-to-school promosPrevious Pixel discounts
SeptemberApple iPhone launchBuy the outgoing iPhone, or carrier trade-in promos
OctoberFestival sales in India; early holiday dealsIndia buyers: watch festival price cuts
Late NovemberBlack Friday and Cyber MondayBroadest discounts across brands and carriers
December 26Boxing Day (Canada, UK, Australia)Strong unlocked and plan promos

Exact dates move every year, so treat this as a guide rather than a guarantee.

Three timing rules

  1. Never buy a phone the month before its successor launches unless the discount is deep.
  2. The best value is often the previous flagship in the first three months after a new launch.
  3. Plan deals peak at year-end and during switching season when carriers chase new lines.

How to know a price is actually good

Compare the sale price with the launch price and how long the phone has been out. Our Good-Deal Checker gives a verdict in seconds, and our live deals feed shows what other shoppers are finding right now.

Do not forget the plan

A $200 phone discount is wiped out by a plan that costs $20 more per month for 24 months. Pair your phone timing with a plan check using the Plan Finder.

FAQ

Is Black Friday really the cheapest time?

It is usually the broadest sale, but launch-season discounts on outgoing models can be just as deep.

Should I preorder a new phone?

Only if the preorder bonus has real value to you. Prices almost always fall later.

How fast do phones lose value?

Flagships commonly lose a large share of their value in the first year, then depreciate more slowly.
