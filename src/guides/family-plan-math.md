Family plans look like an obvious win: the more lines you add, the lower the price per line. But because budget plans have become so cheap, separate prepaid lines can beat a family discount, especially when family members use very different amounts of data.

## Step 1: List each person's real usage

Write down each line's average monthly data. A typical household has one heavy user, one or two medium users and a light user (often a child or older parent on Wi-Fi most of the day).

## Step 2: Price the family plan

Multi-line plans usually charge a total for all lines. Using published Google Fi pricing as an example (checked November 2026), Unlimited Essentials costs $35 for one line and $90 in total for four lines, which is $22.50 per line. The discount is real.

## Step 3: Price a mix-and-match setup

Now price each person on the plan that fits them individually. For example, one unlimited line at $25, two 15GB lines at $20 and one 5GB line at $15 totals **$80 a month**, which is $10 less than the family plan, with every person covered.

## Step 4: Add the hidden factors

- **Shared perks:** streaming bundles or hotspot on a family plan may be worth paying for.
- **One bill vs several:** separate prepaid lines mean more accounts to manage, although some carriers let you group lines.
- **Device deals:** family postpaid plans often unlock multi-line phone promotions.
- **Prepay terms:** some cheapest prices need 12 months paid upfront per line.

## The rule of thumb

- **All heavy users:** a family unlimited plan usually wins.
- **Mixed usage:** mix-and-match prepaid often wins.
- **2 lines:** compare carefully; family discounts are smallest at two lines.

## FAQ

### Can family members be on different networks?
Yes, with separate plans. With a family plan everyone shares one carrier.

### Do kids need unlimited data?
Rarely. A capped plan with Wi-Fi at home and school usually works and limits surprises.

### Is a family plan a credit risk?
The account holder is responsible for every line's charges on postpaid plans.
