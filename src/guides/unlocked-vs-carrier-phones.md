A carrier-locked phone only works with the carrier that sold it until it is unlocked. An unlocked phone works with any compatible network. That single difference shapes how much you will pay for service over the life of the phone.

## Why unlocked usually wins

- **Plan freedom:** move to a cheaper plan the moment one appears. Over two years that flexibility is often worth more than any device discount.
- **Travel:** add a local SIM or travel eSIM abroad instead of paying daily roaming. See our [travel eSIM guide]({{ROOT}}guides/travel-esim-guide/).
- **Resale value:** unlocked phones typically sell for more and to a wider pool of buyers.

## When a carrier phone makes sense

If a carrier promotion gives you a large discount and you were going to stay on that carrier for the whole term, a locked phone costs you nothing extra. Run the comparison in our [True Cost Calculator]({{ROOT}}phones/#true-cost).

## How locking works

Unlock policies differ by carrier and change over time. Typically a phone must be fully paid off and the line active for a minimum period before the carrier unlocks it. Prepaid phones often have longer waiting periods. Always read the current policy on your carrier's website.

## How to check if your phone is unlocked

1. **iPhone:** Settings → General → About. Look for "Carrier Lock: No SIM restrictions."
2. **Android:** try a SIM or eSIM from another carrier, or ask your carrier. Some models show the status under network settings.
3. **Before buying used:** ask the seller for the IMEI, then check its status with the carrier and a blacklist checker. Our [refurbished checklist]({{ROOT}}guides/refurbished-phone-checklist/) covers this.

## Buying unlocked without overpaying

- Buy at launch-cycle discounts. See [the best time to buy]({{ROOT}}guides/best-time-to-buy-a-phone/).
- Consider last year's flagship; the price drop is usually larger than the feature gap.
- Certified refurbished with a 1-year warranty is a strong middle ground.

## FAQ

### Does unlocking void my warranty?
No. Carrier unlocking through official channels does not affect the manufacturer warranty.

### Can any phone be unlocked?
Most can once the carrier's conditions are met. Phones reported lost or stolen, or with unpaid balances, usually cannot.

### Is an unlocked phone the same as a factory-unlocked phone?
A factory-unlocked phone was never locked. A carrier-unlocked phone was locked and released later. Both work on any compatible network.
