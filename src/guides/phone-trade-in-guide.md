Your old phone is worth real money, and the gap between the worst and best offer for the same device is often more than $100. Here is how to capture the top of that range.

## The three routes

| Route | Typical value | Speed | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carrier/retailer trade-in | High when tied to a promo, low otherwise | Instant credit | Low |
| Buyback sites | Medium, compare several | 3–10 days | Low |
| Private sale | Highest | Varies | Highest |

**Carrier trade-ins** can be excellent during launch promotions, but the value usually arrives as bill credits over 24–36 months and requires staying on a qualifying plan. See [bill credits decoded]({{ROOT}}guides/free-phone-bill-credits-explained/).

**Buyback companies** pay cash or PayPal after inspecting the phone. Prices vary a lot between buyers for the same model, which is why comparison sites exist. Some lock a quote for a period of time, so you can ship without losing the price.

**Private sales** on peer-to-peer marketplaces usually pay the most but involve listing, messaging, shipping and fraud risk.

## Seven steps to a higher payout

1. **Sell early.** Values drop fastest right after a new model launches. Sell before or right at launch.
2. **Grade honestly.** Over-grading leads to a revised, lower offer after inspection.
3. **Clean it.** Wipe the screen, clear the ports and remove the case.
4. **Include the box and accessories** if a private buyer is involved.
5. **Pay off and unlock it.** Unlocked phones fetch more and are accepted by more buyers.
6. **Remove accounts.** Sign out of iCloud or Google, disable Find My, and factory reset. A locked account can void the sale.
7. **Compare at least three offers.** Start with our [Trade-in Estimator]({{ROOT}}trade-in/) to know your fair range, then let us collect real offers for you.

## What lowers value the most

- Cracked screen or back glass.
- Battery health below roughly 80%.
- Water damage indicators.
- Account or carrier locks, or a blacklisted IMEI.

## FAQ

### Is it worth repairing a cracked screen before selling?
Sometimes. If the repair costs much less than the value gap between "cracked" and "good" grades, repair first. Get a quote and compare.

### Can I sell a phone that is still on a payment plan?
Many buyers will not accept financed phones, and some pay less. Paying off the balance usually unlocks a better price.

### How do I protect my data?
Back up, sign out of all accounts, remove eSIMs and SIM cards, then factory reset.
