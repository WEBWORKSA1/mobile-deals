A good refurbished phone is the single easiest way to cut the cost of a phone without dropping to a budget model. The key is knowing what "refurbished" means at each seller, because the word is not regulated the same way everywhere.

## The checklist

1. **Seller type.** Manufacturer-certified refurbished is the safest; established marketplaces with seller ratings come next; anonymous listings carry the most risk.
2. **Warranty.** Look for at least 12 months. Many reputable refurbishers now offer a 1-year warranty.
3. **Return window.** 30 days is common and lets you test properly.
4. **Cosmetic grade.** Names vary (Excellent/Good/Fair or A/B/C). Read the seller's definition; "Fair" often means visible scratches.
5. **Battery health.** Ask for the percentage or a guarantee. Below about 80% you will notice shorter battery life.
6. **Unlock status.** Confirm it is unlocked or compatible with your carrier. See [unlocked vs carrier phones]({{ROOT}}guides/unlocked-vs-carrier-phones/).
7. **IMEI clean.** The phone must not be reported lost or stolen or carry an unpaid balance. Ask for the IMEI and check it before paying.
8. **Parts.** Ask whether the screen or battery is original or replaced, and whether replacements are genuine.
9. **Software support.** Check how many years of OS updates the model has left. A cheap phone that stops getting security updates soon is poor value.
10. **Price check.** Compare against new sale prices and our [Good-Deal Checker]({{ROOT}}phones/#deal-check). A refurb should be meaningfully cheaper than a discounted new unit.

## Day-one test routine

- Make a call on speaker and earpiece; test both microphones.
- Test every camera, flash, Face ID or fingerprint, and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth.
- Charge with a cable and wirelessly if supported.
- Check the display for dead pixels and burn-in on a grey background.
- Install your eSIM or SIM and confirm 5G and calls work.

## FAQ

### Is refurbished the same as used?
No. A refurbished phone has been inspected, repaired where needed and tested by the seller; a used phone is sold as-is.

### Which grade gives the best value?
The middle grade usually offers the best price-to-condition ratio. Cosmetic marks do not affect performance.

### Should I buy extended protection?
Only if the cost is small relative to the phone price and covers accidental damage you are likely to have.
