Your three options

Carrier roaming pass

Easiest: your number works as normal. Most expensive on longer trips.

Travel eSIM

Buy before you fly, land connected. Keep your home line on for calls and texts.

Local SIM

Often cheapest per GB for long stays, but needs a shop visit and sometimes ID.

Before you fly: eSIM setup checklist

  1. Confirm your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked.
  2. Install the travel eSIM at home on Wi-Fi; leave its data off until you land.
  3. Label your lines "Home" and "Travel" and set Travel as the data line on arrival.
  4. Keep Home active for calls and texts but turn its data roaming off.
  5. Download offline maps as a backup.

FAQ

Will I keep my phone number abroad?

Yes, if you keep your home line active alongside the travel eSIM. Watch for charges on incoming calls and texts.

How much data do I need per day?

Maps, messaging and light browsing typically use 0.3–0.7GB a day. Streaming and video calls use much more.
